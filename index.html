Crew Members

  • Picard

    Captain Jean-Luc Picard is the thoughtful and diplomatic commander of the USS Enterprise-D.

  • Riker

    Commander William Riker is Picard's trusted First Officer and an experienced starship officer.

  • Data

    Lieutenant Commander Data is an android science officer who seeks to understand humanity.

  • Worf

    Lieutenant Worf is the Enterprise-D's Klingon security chief and tactical officer.

  • Crusher

    Dr Beverly Crusher is the ship's chief medical officer.

  • La Forge

    Lieutenant Commander Geordi La Forge is the ship's chief engineer.

  • Troi

    Commander Deanna Troi is the ship's empathic counsellor.

Ship Division

The crew members of the USS Enterprise-D are divided into three main divisions based on their roles:

Command Division

The command division is responsible for the overall leadership and strategic decision-making of the ship.

Operations Division

The operations division handles the technical and administrative aspects of the ship's operation.

Science and Medical Division

The science and medical division is responsible for scientific research and medical care aboard the ship.
